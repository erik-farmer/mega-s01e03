5 of the parser reports read as a one-day move, not a swap; counted here by tenant, tracked in their own issue.

The problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so a date like 4/7 becomes 7 April instead of 4 July. Most of #4471 is this.

hint: booking-v3's form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People at day-first tenants type month first, as told, and the form reads the date the tenant's way. This happens only through the form, and only at booking-v3 tenants. Fixing the parser does not fix it: the hint is wrong.

one-day move: the date moves by one day, not swapped. The desk noted these in desk-notes.json and then filed them under the parser by tenant. They say "one day off", "the invite arrived before I made the booking", and "Lisbon got Tuesday, Toronto got Monday". A month and day swap cannot do that. It looks like a timezone conversion.

How they were counted

  • Reports are unique by id. The ticket export repeats some reports across overlapping pages.
  • The cause comes from the tenant (causes.ts): its locale and its booking form. intakeTag is intake's guess from the reporter's browser, and it disagrees with the tenant in both directions.
  • The reports in desk-notes.json stay counted under the cause their tenant puts them in, as the desk counts them, and are footnoted there.

Tickets

  • #1 parser: closed, not planned here. The fix is the one queued on #4471.
  • #2 hint: closed, not planned here. It needs its own fix to booking-v3's hint.
  • #3 one-day move: closed as a duplicate of #1, where the desk counts these reports.

Lessons

  • A matching subject line does not mean the same cause. Read the tenant, the channel and the body.
  • A note that says "read differently" is a finding. Give it its own ticket.
